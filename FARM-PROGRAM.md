## Recipe — FARM 1 (build at 325 housing)

- **Troops: 108× Sneaky Goblin (324) + 1× Archer (1) = 325.**
  Growth steps: 330 housing → 110 Sneaky (330 exact). 335 → 111 Sneaky + 2 Archers.
  340 → 113 Sneaky + 1 Archer. (Research comp at 320 was 106 + 2 Archers
  [derived fill of a documented shape, 01-meta-armies §9]; scaled, not re-invented.)
- **Spells (11): 3× Jump (6) + 3× Invisibility (3) + 2× Haste (2).**
  The documented package is Jump + Invisibility [community]; the classic fill runs
  5× Invisibility. This fill trades two Invisibility for Haste on purpose [Fin
  design]: Haste has no precision timing (drop it on a traveling pack), and our
  seats' measured weakness is spell timing. If storage/TH dives underperform in
  the test, revert to the documented 3 Jump + 5 Invisibility fill and re-measure.
- **Siege: Log Launcher (own, L4) by default** — fire-and-forget wall opening on
  the Town Hall line. **Battle Blimp (own, L5) swap when the Clan Castle holds
  goblins** — the documented Town Hall snipe. On a pure perimeter-collector base,
  deploy no siege at all.
- **Heroes: whoever is awake; none required.** Hold heroes for the Town Hall
  finish or the 50% star. This is the heroes-down property the program exists
  for — farming continues through the hero rotation instead of around it.
- **Clan Castle: nothing required.** Seats never request donations (rail). Use
  whatever JJ/clanmates have already filled.

## Target rules

- The **Loot Floor Rule applies as written** (screen offer ≥600k G AND ≥600k E,
  or ≥8,000 DE; 10-Next budget, then best seen). Test metric logged per raid:
  **taken ÷ offered** — goblin extraction is collector-weighted, so if the block
  shows a systematic <50% extraction of screen offers, Fin recalibrates a
  sneaky-specific floor from the data rather than letting seats Next past good
  collector bases or engage bad ones.
- **Base shape matters more than the number:** engage when the loot sits in
  collectors on the perimeter or behind the first wall layer. A rich base whose
  loot is mostly deep storages is an Army 1 base — Next it.

## Deployment procedure (seat doctrine for the test)

1. Read first: which collectors are full, where the loot layer sits, Town Hall
   position. Zoom fully out before deploying (standing procedure).
2. Deploy in **packs of 5–10 per collector cluster**, working around the
   perimeter. Never dump the army on one point (splash wipe + overkill waste).
3. Jump goes on the wall line **before** the pack arrives. Invisibility only on
   a pack actively diving storages or the Town Hall. Haste on packs traveling
   between distant clusters.
4. Town Hall: take it when it's on a goblin path, down the Log Launcher lane,
   or Blimp-able. Do not burn the army chasing a deep Town Hall — the next
   raid's collectors are worth more than one star. Star Bonus stars accumulate
   across the day.
5. **End the battle when the accessible loot is drained.** Surrendering around
   40–60% is the design, not a failure. Loot per hour is the objective.
6. Report every raid: opponent, offered vs taken (G/E/DE), spells used, Town
   Hall taken or not, battle duration, trophies start/end of block.
