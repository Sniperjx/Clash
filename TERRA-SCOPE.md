## D. Donations — bounded flow (Sol-reviewed)

   OPEN clan chat to view requests — viewing is not a social action. Sending any

2. The exact troop is already trained and surplus to Army-v1 readiness (if S1-B

   trial troops). NEVER quick-train to donate. NEVER spend gems to donate.
3. [LIVE CHECK x3] — Sol reviews a screenshot of the request AND the donate screen

4. Donate exactly the requested troop and count. Nothing extra, nothing substituted.
- STOP: ambiguous request, troop not trained, any gem cost, anything requiring
  typing -> abort and log.
