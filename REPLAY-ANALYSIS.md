# Replay analysis loop (standing, 2026-11-18)
Purpose: turn battle replays into doctrine changes without trusting any single model's eyes.
Loop: (1) a replay is recorded (JJ, or a seat where capture is available); (2) Gemini analyzes it under the prompt of record (~/workspace/clash-army-research/07-gemini-replay-analysis-prompt.md — one replay per fresh chat); (3) Fin scores the report: its GROUND TRUTH block must match a battle in the Attack Log / SESSION-LOG (opponent, stars, %, loot) or the entire analysis is VOID, however plausible its timeline sounds; (4) surviving observations become doctrine candidates in ATTACK-PLAN, labeled with their source; (5) a candidate is adopted only after it is consistent with the operator's own notes or a second case.
Trust rules: Gemini's known failure mode is confident invention; the ground-truth gate exists because of it. Internal timestamp contradictions downgrade a report even when its ground truth matches. No dial-in ever ships from an unverified analysis.
